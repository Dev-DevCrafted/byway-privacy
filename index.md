# Byway privacy policy

**Effective date:** 24 October 2026

Byway is an Android app made by DevCrafted for controlling 1:76 scale model road vehicles over Bluetooth. This policy explains what information the app handles and what happens to it.

## Summary

Byway does not collect, upload or share any personal data. Everything the app stores stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

## Information stored on your phone

To work, Byway keeps the following on your device, in the app's private storage:

- **Your vehicles:** the name, icon and colour you give each vehicle, its Bluetooth module's name and address, whether the module uses a PIN (never the PIN itself), its motor minimum setting, and its last known status
- **Activity logs:** the commands sent to your vehicles, their replies and events, kept for the period you choose in Settings
- **App settings:** your preferences, such as theme, polling interval and log retention

None of this is sent to DevCrafted or anyone else. Android's cloud backup is turned off for Byway, so it isn't copied to your Google account either.

## Permissions

- **Nearby devices (Bluetooth):** to find and connect to your vehicles
- **Location (Android 11 and earlier only):** older versions of Android require location permission before an app can scan for Bluetooth devices. Byway uses it only to scan and never reads your location
- **Notifications:** for fault, battery and connection alerts, and the ongoing notification while vehicles are connected
- **Foreground service:** to keep your vehicles connected and monitored while the screen is off
- **Vibration:** for haptic feedback on controls such as STOP ALL
- **Network access:** included by the app framework. Byway makes no network requests of its own. The only online actions are links in the About screen, which open in your web browser

## Sharing

Byway never shares your data. If you choose to export a log, the app creates a text or CSV file and opens Android's share sheet. You decide which app it goes to and who receives it. Once it has been sent, that app's own privacy policy applies.

## Deleting your data

- **Forget a vehicle** in its settings to remove its record, its log and its Android pairing
- **Clear the logs** at any time from Settings, or set how long they are kept
- **Uninstall Byway**, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything

## Children

Byway is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
